# Research brief

Parallel research streams that merge into a single briefing document.

## Collect sources
Gather 8-12 recent, credible sources on the topic: docs, papers,
reputable news, primary sources. Prefer primary over secondary.
Output a bullet list with one-line summaries and URLs.

## Extract key claims
depends: collect-sources
From the collected sources, extract the 5-7 most important claims
or findings. Note which sources support each claim and where they
disagree.

## Find counterarguments
depends: collect-sources
Actively look for credible counterarguments or limitations to the
main claims. Steelman the opposing view.

## Draft brief
depends: extract-key-claims, find-counterarguments
Write a balanced 500-word briefing: context, key claims with
evidence, counterarguments, and open questions. No hype.
